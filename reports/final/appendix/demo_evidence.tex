\section{Road-Show Evidence Map}
\label{app:demo-evidence}

The road-show package is recorder-ready rather than a completed video. Readiness is evidenced by timed narration, a shot list, fallback assets, and claim-boundary notes that keep the three-minute recording tied to implemented artifacts instead of invented screen claims.
